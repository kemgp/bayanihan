BAYANIHAN is a proposed web app for booking local handymen. Customers confirm completed work and authorize payment; Paiflow splits it between the worker and platform.

Basis: supplied Pitch Deck.deck, paiflow-guide.pdf, event-primer.pdf and setup-guide.pdf, plus the feasibility plan. Three contributors using AI-assisted development. October 24, 2026. Product choices are proposals; all demo payments use Stellar Testnet. Physical job completion is simulated in the demo.
References: Paiflow starter · SDK documentation

The central idea

Skilled help, close to home.

Book a local handyman, agree on the work and price, then pay after confirming completion. Paiflow automatically sends 85% to the worker and 15% to BAYANIHAN.
